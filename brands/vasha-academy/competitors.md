## Notes per competitor

### 1. Banglay IELTS

- **Why it's relevant:** direct competitor on both the mock-test and course offers. It publishes a lot of
  SEO articles on "IELTS computer based mock test in BD", "online mock test" and "IELTS writing test
  checker" [https://banglayielts.com/?p=44998, https://banglayielts.com/?p=48102, https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=47645].
- **Offer:** physical centres in Dhaka, Chittagong and Sylhet plus online courses
  [https://banglayielts.com/?p=44664]. It says its online mock tests are "accessible anywhere in Bangladesh", with Writing
  and Speaking evaluation [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=44508].
- **Positioning against AI:** its pages argue that AI grading only gives an estimate and that human
  expert feedback is "essential" [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=45558]. Expect this argument against
  Vasha's AI marking. (Vasha's brief already calls AI scores "practice estimates", which fits.)
- **Typical course length:** 2–4 months [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=46221]. Mock-test price: unknown.
- **Formats, engagement, active ads:** unknown (Facebook and Ad Library not accessible).

### 2. 10 Minute School, IELTS Course by Munzereen Shahid

- **Why it's relevant:** the biggest Bangla-language online education brand. Its IELTS course competes for
  the same self-study, phone-first audience.
- **Offer:** a recorded course with 25 video lectures, 10 Reading and 10 Listening mock tests, 38 lecture
  sheets and a Facebook support group with weekly Zoom classes [https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course, via search].
  Its mock tests cover Reading and Listening only [https://10minuteschool.com/en/ielts/, via search]. Price: unknown.
- **Instructor-led brand:** Munzereen Shahid (MSc English, Oxford; IELTS 8.5). Her page grew past 700k
  followers in nine months [Prothom Alo link above]. The face of the instructor is the brand.
- **Possible gap for Vasha** (inferred): no Writing or Speaking mock tests were mentioned in the sources, so
  Vasha's AI Writing/Speaking marking may fill a gap for this audience.
- **Formats, engagement, active ads:** unknown.

### 3. FutureEd

- **Why it's relevant:** a British Council test venue and coaching centre that uses a **free mock test** as
  its lead offer.
- **Offer:** a free mock test covering Reading, Listening and Writing in paper and computer-delivered formats,
  plus a 1:1 Speaking test with a teacher. Feedback comes within 3 days [https://futureed.org/ielts-mock-test, via search].
  Vasha's AI result in about 5 minutes is much faster (comparison inferred from the brief's USP).
- **Scale claim:** "15,000+ IELTS candidates every year" (self-reported) [https://futureed.org/ielts-preparation-course, via search].
- **Location:** Ahmed Tower, Banani, Dhaka [https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-futureed-ahmed-tower-banani-9th-floor].
- **Note for the brief's no-go rules:** FutureEd *is* an official test venue. Vasha must not imply the same status.
- **Facebook page, formats, engagement, active ads:** unknown.

### 4. OnMock

- **Why it's relevant:** the closest product match. It sells AI-evaluated IELTS mock tests across all four modules,
  "designed for Bangladeshi learners", with instant band-score feedback [https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/onmock.com].
- **Pricing:** "unlimited practice at an affordable price". The exact figure is unknown [Trustpilot listing above].
- **Scale claim:** "1,000+ premium users", self-published by its developer (Sylhet-based)
  [https://suhan.vercel.app/project-OnMock.html]. Unverified.
- **Facebook page, formats, engagement, active ads:** unknown.

### 5. Bandscore9

- **Why it's relevant:** an AI-led IELTS prep platform in Dhaka, with an "AI Speaking Teacher", hundreds of tests,
  a website plus app, low pricing and hybrid 1-on-1 mentorship
  [https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/352036/bangladesh-s-first-ai-powered-ielts-preparation; https://observerbd.com/news.php?id=481267].
- **Launch:** July 2024, Gulshan 1 [Dhaka Tribune above]. It calls itself "Bangladesh's first AI-powered IELTS
  preparation" platform, which competes directly with Vasha's AI marking USP.
- **Current activity:** unknown. The latest coverage found is from 2024, so check whether it is still active.
- **Facebook page, formats, engagement, active ads:** unknown.

### 6. IELTSly (added 2026-11-07)

- **Why it's relevant:** an AI mock-test platform advertising on "IELTS mock test" in Bangladesh; the
  closest product match to Vasha's mock tests.
- **Offer seen in ads:** "Unlimited Mock Test সাথে Instant Result এখন মাত্র ৯৯০ টাকায়"
