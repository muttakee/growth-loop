# Targeting: Vasha Academy, 2026-W46

- **Dates:** Mon 9 Nov to Sun 15 Nov 2026
- **Inputs read:** `brief.md`, `personas.md`, `memory.md`, `weeks/2026-W46/research.md`
- **Binding constraints applied:** hooks in Bangla (IELTS terms in English); friendly older-sibling voice ("তুমি");
  no "guaranteed band" or any score promise; never "official"; AI Writing/Speaking scores are always a
  **practice estimate**; no competitor named or attacked; no visa or immigration advice; no fake results;
  **no official exam fee figure** in any hook (user decision, memory experiment 1); mock tests are **Academic only**
  (General Training candidates go to the IELTS course).

## Focus personas

| Persona | Why this week | Main pain point | Desire |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|--------|
| **Mim, the HSC 2026 graduate** (new Persona 4, added this week) | HSC results came out 31 Oct and week 2 after results is the decision window. They don't know what IELTS involves or where to start, and they worry their English isn't good enough to begin. | A clear, low-pressure first step, and a free way to see where they stand before anyone (or their parents) pays for coaching. |
| **Rafi, the master's applicant checking his band** (Persona 1) | Computer-delivered is now the only IELTS format in Bangladesh, and CD/typing nerves are an active ad theme for two big advertisers (see research: Top 3 findings #3; Niche news, "Computer-delivered only"; Active ads, FutureEd CD reassurance). This is Vasha's core USP (an interface that looks like the real computer-delivered IELTS, results in minutes) and lets us run memory experiments 1 and 2. | Booking the expensive exam before he's ready, and walking into a computer-based test whose screen he has never used. | Know where his band is now, for ৳20, and sit the real test with the screen already familiar. |
| **Sakib, the retaker stuck half a band short** (Persona 3) | Writing stuck at 5–5.5 is being used as a lead-magnet hook in paid ads, and the only real audience doubt we have on AI marking is that it "flatters" the user (see research: Audience questions and language, AI doubt and pain phrases; Active ads, FutureEd Writing lead magnet). **Not in focus this week: Tania (Persona 2, the job seeker).** Nothing in this week's research is specific to her, and her
route usually needs General Training, which our mock tests don't cover yet. One angle can still reach her through the
IELTS course or SpeakUp, but the Planner shouldn't push Academic mocks at her.

## Notes for the Planner

- **Don't** make "Mid-Nov prediction" content; it implies inside knowledge of the exam (research: Niche news, prediction posts).
- **Don't** copy the "1 month to Band 7+" or "band guarantee" patterns seen in competitor ads (research: Competitor standouts,
  FutureEd #2; Active ads, British Council); both break the brief.
- Students' results can't be used unless they are real and consented (research: Banglay IELTS Sylhet result post).
- Competitors run WhatsApp CTAs on problem-first Bangla copy (research: Active ads). Our CTA is the mock-test link; the user decides
  whether to add a messaging CTA.
- IELTSly (new AI mock platform, ৳990 unlimited) is the closest match to our offer. Whether to add it to `competitors.md` is the
  user's call (research: Niche news).
