## Profile

### Mission

Help Bangladeshi students reach their target IELTS band with affordable, realistic practice,
through IELTS courses and IELTS mock tests.

### Vision

The most trusted IELTS prep platform in Bangladesh, and the biggest seller of IELTS mock tests.

### Next year goal

Sell **15,000 mock tests** and enrol **1,500 course students** in the next 12 months.

### USP

- A real computer-based exam interface that looks and feels like the real computer-delivered IELTS.
- Instant band scores for Listening and Reading.
- AI marking for Writing and Speaking against all four official IELTS criteria, with results in
  about 5 minutes.

### Target customer

Bangladeshi students aged 18–30 preparing for IELTS to study abroad, plus job seekers going to
the UK, Canada or Australia.

### Customer persona

**Rafi, 22, final-year university student in Dhaka.** Needs an overall band 6.5 (no band below
6.0) for a UK master's admission. Writing and Speaking worry him most because he has no one to
give honest feedback. He studies mostly on his phone in the evenings and watches free IELTS
videos on Facebook and YouTube. Coaching centres feel too expensive and slow. Before he pays for
the official exam (about ৳25,000+), he wants to know his real band score so he doesn't waste the
fee. He buys when he sees proof the mock test feels like the real computer-delivered exam, and
when results come fast.

## Content rules

### Language

- **Main language for captions, hooks, voiceovers and calls to action:** Bangla.
- **Content that stays in another language:** IELTS terms (band, Task 1, Task 2, Lexical
  Resource, etc.) and sample answers stay in English.
- **Quality rule:** must sound natural, not machine-translated.

### Voice

- A friendly older sibling: encouraging and practical.
- **Avoid:** "guaranteed band" (and any similar promise of a score).

### Visuals and audio

- **Logo placement:** top-left.
- **Colours and fonts:** taken from the vasha.ac theme.
  - Primary coral red `#FA4542` / `#FF474B`
  - Dark red `#C50023` / `#8C0000`
  - Dark green accent `#02302B` / `#024239`
  - White background
  - Fonts: **Hind Siliguri** (Bangla), **Plus Jakarta Sans** (English)
- **Music:** allowed (royalty-free background music).
- **People and figures:** real instructors in Reels. No stock photos of foreigners.
- **Preferred visuals:** clean and minimal, a bold Bangla headline, screenshots of the mock test
  interface and band score reports, coral-red accents.

### Approved sources

None. The brand does not quote sources.

### No-go topics

- Never promise or imply a "guaranteed band".
- Never claim to be official, or affiliated with or endorsed by the British Council, IDP IELTS or
  Cambridge University Press & Assessment. "Looks like the real computer-delivered IELTS" is fine;
  "official" is not.
- AI scores for Writing and Speaking are always described as **practice estimates**, never as an
  official IELTS result.
- No attacking other coaching centres by name.
- No visa or immigration advice.
- No fake student results or testimonials.
