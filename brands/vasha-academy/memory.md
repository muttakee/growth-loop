## Competitor insights

<!-- Formats that stand out for competitors (inspiration only, never copy) -->

(onboarding, 2026-11-04) Facebook pages and the Meta Ad Library could not be opened, so these come from
the competitors' websites and press coverage, not from their posts. No engagement numbers yet.

- **The free mock test is the standard lead offer.** FutureEd offers a free Reading, Listening and Writing mock plus a 1:1
  Speaking test, with feedback in 3 days [https://futureed.org/ielts-mock-test]. Vasha's free Listening
  Tests 01–02 can compete, with results in minutes instead of days (comparison inferred from the brief).
- **Search content on "computer-based mock test".** Banglay IELTS publishes many articles on "IELTS
  computer based mock test BD" [https://banglayielts.com/?p=44998, https://banglayielts.com/?p=48102].
  Demand for the topic likely rose after paper-based IELTS ended in Bangladesh on 31 Jan 2026
  [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/which-test-do-i-take/computer-based-ielts] (inferred).
- **"AI is only an estimate" counter-messaging.** Banglay IELTS says human feedback is essential
  [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=45558]. Vasha should own the honest framing ("practice estimate", per the
  brief) rather than overclaim.
- **Instructor-as-brand.** 10 Minute School's IELTS course is sold on Munzereen Shahid's credentials (Oxford,
  IELTS 8.5) and her large personal following [https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/good-day-bangladesh/munzereen-studies-english-and-teaches-english-too].
  This supports using Nishi & Kamil in Reels, as the brief requires.
- **AI-first rivals exist.** OnMock (all four modules, AI marking) [https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/onmock.com] and
  Bandscore9 ("Bangladesh's first AI-powered IELTS preparation platform") [https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/352036/bangladesh-s-first-ai-powered-ielts-preparation].
  "AI marking" alone is not unique. The realistic interface plus the ৳20 price may be the sharper angle (inferred).

   *Confirmed if* it gets at least 1.5x the page's median reach and more link clicks to /en/ielts-mock than the

2. **Interface screen-recording vs talking-head instructor Reel.** Run one of each in the same week on the
