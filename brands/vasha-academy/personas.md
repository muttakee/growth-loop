## Persona 1: Rafi, the master's applicant checking his real band (from the brief)

- **Who:** 21–24, final-year university student or fresh graduate, Dhaka. Applying for a UK master's
  [brief.md, Customer persona].
- **Goal:** an overall band 6.5 with no band below 6.0, on the first attempt [brief.md].
- **Fears and pain points:**
  - Writing and Speaking scare him most, and he has no one to give him honest feedback [brief.md].
  - Wasting the official IELTS exam fee by booking before he's ready [brief.md].
  - Coaching feels too expensive and slow [brief.md]. Dhaka coaching costs about ৳8,000–25,000+
    [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=46842].
  - Paper-based IELTS has ended in Bangladesh (IDP, from 31 Jan 2026), so he *must* sit the computer
    test and the interface is new to him [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/which-test-do-i-take/computer-based-ielts].
- **Words they use (inferred):** "আমার আসল band কত আসবে?", "writing এ কেউ check করে দেয় না",
  "computer based exam এ কি টাইপিং এ সমস্যা হবে?", "exam এর টাকা নষ্ট করতে চাই না".
- **When they're online:** evenings on his phone [brief.md]. Probably 9 pm–1 am (inferred).
- **Best hooks:**
  - "Exam fee দেওয়ার আগে ৳20 দিয়ে নিজের band জেনে নাও" (a ৳20 mock vs the cost of the real exam).
  - A screen recording of the mock interface: "এটাই computer-delivered IELTS এর মতো দেখতে".
  - "Writing Task 2 লিখো, ৫ মিনিটে চার criteria তে practice estimate".
- **What makes them buy:** proof the mock feels like the real computer-delivered exam, plus fast results
  [brief.md]. Free Listening Tests 01–02 lower the risk of trying [brief.md, Business product].
- **Update (2026-W46):** nerves about the computer-delivered interface and typing are now a live paid-ad theme
  for two large advertisers, which confirms this pain point is active in the market. The wording in those ads is
  advertiser copy, not audience language [https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1433183992073593],
  [https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1072603158685673].

## Persona 2: Tania, the job seeker going abroad for work

- **Who:** 25–30, working professional or recent graduate, Dhaka or Chattogram. Preparing for IELTS to
  move to the UK, Canada or Australia for work [brief.md, Target customer]. Studies after office hours (inferred).
- **Goal:** reach the band her pathway asks for, quickly, while still working (inferred). Vasha must not
  advise on visa or immigration [brief.md, No-go topics].
- **Fears and pain points:**
  - No time for an 8-week coaching schedule; she needs short, flexible practice (inferred).
  - Unsure if her English from university is "rusty" (inferred).
  - **Product fit:** job and migration routes often need IELTS *General Training*. Vasha's mock tests are
    Academic only, so for her, promote the IELTS course (covers Academic and General Training) [brief.md].
- **Words they use (inferred):** "অফিস করে IELTS এর প্রস্তুতি কিভাবে নিব?", "General নাকি Academic?",
  "speaking এ আটকে যাই", "অল্প সময়ে কিভাবে band বাড়াবো".
- **When they're online:** commute times (8–9 am, 6–8 pm) and late night (10 pm–12 am) (inferred).
- **Best hooks:**
  - "অফিসের পর ৩০ মিনিট: আজকের Reading test".
  - SpeakUp, the 30-day Speaking Challenge at ৳199: "দিনে একটা speaking prompt" [brief.md].
  - Short Speaking Part 2 cue-card practice reels with real instructors [brief.md, Visuals].
- **What makes them buy:** a low price, flexible timing and a clear plan that fits her schedule.
  The ৳150 Reading Bundle and the ৳199 SpeakUp challenge are low-risk first purchases [brief.md].

## Persona 3: Sakib, the retaker stuck half a band short

- **Who:** 22–28, Sylhet or Chattogram. Already took IELTS and scored about 6.0 overall, with Writing
  at 5.5 (inferred). Re-sitting for university admission. Lives outside Dhaka, where coaching
  options are fewer (inferred).
- **Goal:** get Writing from 5.5 to 6.0–6.5 and pass the retake on the next attempt (inferred).
- **Fears and pain points:**
  - Paying the full official exam fee again (inferred).
  - He doesn't know *which* criterion is pulling him down (Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion,
    Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy) (inferred).
  - Distrust of AI marking. Competitors argue that AI only estimates and human feedback is essential
    [https://admin.banglayielts.com/?p=45558].
- **Words they use (inferred):** "writing এ ৫.৫ এর উপরে উঠতে পারছি না", "আবার exam দিতে হবে",
  "কোন criteria তে নাম্বার কাটছে বুঝি না", "Task 2 তে কি ভুল করছি?".
- **When they're online:** late night, 10 pm–1 am (inferred).
- **Best hooks:**
  - "৫.৫ থেকে ৬.৫: চারটা criteria তে কোথায় নাম্বার কাটে" (a carousel with an English sample answer).
  - A before/after Writing Task 2 *practice estimate* screenshot (only real, consented examples, never
    fabricated [brief.md, No-go topics]).
  - The "Nishi & Kamil" live course as the next step after the mocks (৳15,000, target band 6.5–8) [brief.md].
- **What makes them buy:** a breakdown by criterion that shows exactly what to fix, plus the cheap
  per-test price (৳20 for Writing) so he can test, fix and test again [brief.md].
- **Update (2026-W46), words they use (sourced):** a real user doubt about AI marking, quoted by a competitor:
  "আমি ভেবেছিলাম ফ্ল্যাটার করছে ইউজারকে খুশি করতে..." (OnMock page, 22 Sep 2026) [https://www.facebook.com/onmock].
  Answer it with the honest "practice estimate" framing from the brief, never with an accuracy claim.

## Persona 4: Mim, the HSC 2026 graduate (added 2026-W46, kept by the user on 2026-11-07)

- **Who:** 18–19, just passed HSC 2026 (results published 31 Oct 2026; pass rate 52.12%, 40,084 GPA-5)
  [https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/hdjdl19opa], [https://bdnews24.com/education/wyevx1rrka].
  Inside the brief's 18–30 target range [brief.md, Target customer]. Thinking about a bachelor's abroad (inferred).
- **Goal:** work out the next step after HSC and, if IELTS is part of it, start without wasting money (inferred).
- **Fears and pain points:**
  - Doesn't know what IELTS involves or where to start (inferred).
  - Worries their English isn't good enough to begin (inferred).
  - Family pressure and post-result stress; about half of candidates did not pass this year, so the tone must be kind
    [https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/hdjdl19opa] (tone rule inferred).
  - **Market context:** competitors target this group right now with HSC discounts
    [https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2151950825356808], [https://www.facebook.com/reel/961700186623610/].
- **Words they use (sourced, comments on a competitor's HSC reel, names not stored):** "Ami nijei HSC-2026...peray asi...😅",
  "4.33 এ যাত্রায় বেঁচে গেছি", "কে কে পাস করছেন হাত তোলেন🙂" [https://www.facebook.com/reel/2466899444071263/].
  Playful, often Banglish (Bangla in Latin script).
- **When they're online:** not measured yet. Probably late evening and night (inferred).
- **Best hooks:** a free first step (IELTS 101 class, free Listening Tests 01–02), "3 easy steps to start IELTS"
