## Competitor insights

<!-- Formats that stand out for competitors (inspiration only, never copy) -->

From onboarding (2026-11-01). Based on competitor websites and press only. Their Facebook and
Instagram posts and Ad Library entries could not be opened yet, so there are no engagement numbers.

- **Free mock test as the entry offer** is standard (Mentors', FutureEd, 10 Minute School)
  [https://unb.com.bd/category/Lifestyle/free-websites-for-online-ielts-mock-tests/131838;
  https://futureed.org/ielts-mock-test]. Ours must stand out as a *live, on-Facebook, personal
  feedback* speaking test (inferred).
- **One teacher as the face of the brand works at scale:** Munzereen Shahid passed 700,000 Facebook
  followers within 9 months of launching (2020)
  [https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/good-day-bangladesh/munzereen-studies-english-and-teaches-english-too],
  and her 10MS IELTS course shows 34,750 enrolled [https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course, via search snippet].
- **"In Bangla" positioning is crowded** ("ঘরে বসে IELTS প্রস্তুতি", "Bangla-friendly", "Banglay IELTS")
  [https://othoba.com/ghore-boshe-ielts-prostuti-by-munzereen-shahid-book-vandar-521695;
  https://futureed.org/online-courses/haater-muthoi-ielts; https://oneielts.com/ielts-courses/banglay-ielts-crash-course].
  Lead with the coach's story and live practice, not just "in Bangla" (inferred).
- **Timely fee news pulls attention:** Mentors' publishes test-fee updates (BDT 31,100 from 4 Sept 2026)
  [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update]. A "retake costs BDT 31,100, so get it right the
  first time" angle fits our mission (inferred).
- **Community add-ons** (Facebook support group at 10MS, Telegram group at OneIELTS) [links above]
  are expected by buyers (inferred).

## Open experiments

<!-- Ideas being tested and what result would confirm them -->

From onboarding (2026-11-01). Not started yet.

1. **Live mock-test clips beat tip cards.** Cut 30–60 second reels from the weekly Facebook Live
   speaking mock test (a student answer, then the coach's fix, with consent) and compare them with
   static tip carousels on the same topic.
   *Confirmed if:* clips get at least 1.5x the shares and saves of the carousels over 3 weeks, and
   lead to more mock-test bookings.
2. **Fear-first hooks beat score-first hooks.** Same post, two hooks: a fear hook ("Speaking এ ভয়
   লাগে?") against a goal hook ("Band 7 এর জন্য Speaking"), for Personas 1 and 3.
   *Confirmed if:* the fear hook gets at least 30% more comments and a higher 3-second view rate
   across 4 pairs.
3. **Retake-cost angle drives bookings.** Posts that frame the free mock test against the BDT 31,100
   test fee ("retake এর আগে একবার চেক করে নাও"), with no score promises.
   *Confirmed if:* these posts produce more free mock-test sign-ups per 1,000 reach than the
   average of other offer posts over 2–3 weeks.
