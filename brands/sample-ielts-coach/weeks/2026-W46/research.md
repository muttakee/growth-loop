## Top 3 findings

1. **The test fee figure we planned to use is disputed. Check it before any "retake cost" post.**
   Mentors' says the fee rose from BDT 29,600 to **BDT 31,100** (UKVI BDT 33,200 to BDT 34,850)
   for registrations from 4 Sept 2026 [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update]. But
   IDP Bangladesh pages, as indexed, show different and conflicting numbers: BDT 28,450 (IELTS) and
   BDT 27,450 (UKVI) on one page [https://www.idp.com/bangladesh/ielts/prepare-for-ielts/test-fee],
   and a single BDT 24,950 for computer and paper on another
   [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/ielts-exam-fee]. A NextStep page gives a range of
   BDT 23,000–25,500 [https://nextstepielts.com/ielts-exam-fee-in-bangladesh/]. The IDP pages may
   simply be out of date in the index (inferred). Memory experiment 3 ("retake costs BDT 31,100")
   depends on this number. **Planner:** before using any fee in a post, the human should confirm
   it on the British Council or IDP booking page, or use wording without an exact figure.
2. **"One Skill Retake" plus computer-only testing: a timely, no-promise explainer topic.**
   British Council launched IELTS One Skill Retake in Bangladesh (Sept 2023): retake one section
   instead of the whole test, booked within 60 days of the original test, computer-delivered
   Academic/General Training only [https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/326390/british-council-ielts-launches-one-skill-retake-in;
   https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/ae0152r00g; https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/ielts-recognising-organisations/one-skill-retake].
   IDP stopped IELTS on Paper in Bangladesh after 31 Jan 2026, so computer delivery is now the
   main format [https://speakshark.com/blog/ielts-test-dates-november-2026-bangladesh, via search snippet].
   No Bangladesh One Skill Retake fee was found anywhere. Content idea (inferred): "Computer IELTS:
   কী বদলায়, কী বদলায় না" and "One Skill Retake কী, কখন কাজে লাগে". This fits the mission (fewer
   costly full retakes) without any band promise. Facts must be checked on ielts.org, the brief's
   approved source.
3. **Deadline timing: UK January 2027 intake and the admission season both land this week.**
   - Agent guides say Bangladeshi applicants for UK January intake should be well underway by
     October, and they advise against waiting until November. They also say IELTS for UKVI is the
     version needed for the visa (standard IELTS is not accepted for it)
     [https://ardentoverseas.com/january-intake-in-uk/; https://www.gradding.com/blog/study-abroad/january-intake-in-the-uk, via search snippets].
     Oxford Brookes closed UG/Foundation applications from Bangladesh for January 2026 because of
     high demand [https://www.brookes.ac.uk/study/international-students/applying-to-arriving/next-steps, via search snippet].
     This supports a "UKVI নাকি Academic? কোনটা লাগবে" post for Persona 1 (inferred).
   - Dhaka University 2026–27 undergraduate applications open **11 Nov, 12:00pm** and close 25 Nov
     [https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/dhaka-university-admission-applications-open-nov-11-2026-27-session-4238521].
     Rajshahi University runs 12–27 Nov and Chittagong University 15 Nov–10 Dec
     [https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/print/ru-admission-applications-open-nov-12-1786900985; jagonews24 / Dhaka Tribune via search snippets].
     These are HSC leavers, younger than our 19–30 target. They are only a side audience: students
     choosing between local admission and studying abroad (inferred).

- **UK English rules (for Persona 2, work/migration):** the Skilled Worker, Scale-up and HPI English
  requirement rose from B1 to B2 for applications from 8 Jan 2026
  [https://latitudelaw.com/news/new-english-language-threshold-for-many-uk-work-visas; https://www.gov.uk/government/news/migrants-will-be-required-to-pass-a-level-standard-of-english, via search snippets].

  [https://immigrationbarrister.co.uk/new-year-new-rules-key-uk-immigration-changes-for-2026/, via search snippet].
  Degree-level Student visa stays at B2. No Bangladesh-specific change was found.
  Content must stay about the English level. **No visa promises** (brief no-go).
- **Canada study permits:** the 2026 cap is 155,000 new permits, about 49% below 2025. ICEF reports
  150,000 for 2027, but other sources say the 2027 figure is not final
  [https://monitor.icef.com/?p=46376; https://www.mavenconsultingservices.com/article/canada-study-permit-cap/].
  Fewer places means more competition and higher score targets at some universities (inferred).
  Treat this as background only; it needs no post of its own.
- **November test availability:** a third-party guide reports Dhaka computer sessions on 10, 11,
  12 and 14 Nov, and Chattogram on 9 and 14 Nov. It notes that slots change live
  [https://speakshark.com/blog/ielts-test-dates-november-2026-bangladesh, via search snippet].
  Not verified on the official booking pages.
