## Top 3 findings

1. **IELTS in Bangladesh is now computer-only, and One Skill Retake exists.** IDP says the paper test
   is no longer offered in Bangladesh after 31 Jan 2026 [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/which-test-do-i-take/paper-based-ielts, via search snippet];
   IELTS says the UKVI test is offered only in fully digital format [https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery, via search snippet].
   British Council launched One Skill Retake in Bangladesh (retake one section, not the whole test)
   [https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/education/news/british-council-ielts-launches-one-skill-retake-bangladesh-3428586],
   computer-delivered only, booked within 60 days of the original test [https://www.ielts.org/one-skill-retake, via search snippet].
   Angles for us: "computer-delivered IELTS: what's different on the day" and an honest explainer of
   One Skill Retake. Note for the Planner: One Skill Retake weakens the "a retake costs the full
   BDT 31,100" framing in memory.md experiment 3; its Bangladesh fee was not found (inferred impact).
2. **The test fee figure is not settled. Check it before any post quotes it.** Mentors' says fees rose
   to BDT 31,100 (UKVI BDT 34,850) for registrations from 4 Sept 2026 [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update, via search snippet],
   but IDP's own pages (as shown in search snippets) list BDT 28,450 / UKVI 27,450
   [https://www.idp.com/bangladesh/ielts/prepare-for-ielts/test-fee] and BDT 24,950
   [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/ielts-exam-fee], and a third-party snapshot shows British
   Council Dhaka October sessions at BDT 29,600 [https://speakshark.com/blog/ielts-exam-dates-october-2026-bangladesh].
   The IDP pages may be out of date (inferred). Use "about BDT 31,000" only once confirmed on the
   official booking page.
3. **UK January 2027 intake deadlines fall in November.** Kent: funds transfer deadline 16 Nov,
   application deadline 27 Nov; LJMU: CAS issuing deadline 30 Nov 2026
   [https://www.gradding.com/blog/study-abroad/january-intake-in-the-uk, via search snippet;
   https://cm-prod.ljmu.ac.uk/campaigns/january-intake-2027]. (Kent dates are given without a year in
   the snippet; that they are for the Jan 2027 intake is inferred.) UK Student visa English level rose from
   CEFR B1 to B2 from 8 Jan 2026 (secondary source; check gov.uk)
   [https://www.sableinternational.com/blog/uk-immigration-rules-to-change-for-2026-and-2027, via search snippet].
   Fits Persona 1 (UK/Canada final-year student): "January intake? Your IELTS timeline this month",
   with no visa promises.
