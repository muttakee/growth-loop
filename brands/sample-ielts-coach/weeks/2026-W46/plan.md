  bookings (the main offer), toward the next-year goal of 300 paid batch students and 20,000
  followers. Judge organic posts on comments and shares, not views (targeting: Notes for the Planner).

    "in Bangla" (`fri-01-reel`, `sun-01-live`).

  - **Comment replies in Bangla only** (no Banglish), in the elder-brother voice.
  - **No results or testimonial posts** (Content interview has none with consent).
  - **No UKVI and no "General vs Academic" posts** this week.
  - **Bangladesh only:** no visa, immigration, work-route or university-intake content (brief:
    No-go topics).

## Post details

### mon-01-carousel
- **Pillar / theme:** Authority / open
- **Persona and pain point:** P3, believes one weak module means retaking the whole test
- **Hook:** "একটা module খারাপ হলে পুরো IELTS আবার দিতে হয়।" সবসময় না। চলো দেখি।
- **Idea:** The week's main "retake options" post (angle 1, figure-free). Exam-style cards: what One
  Skill Retake is; when it fits (one weak module, rules met); when it does **not** fit (paper test,
  more than one weak module, outside the time window); what a full retake means; last card:
  "আগে জানো কোন module-এ নম্বর কাটছে: রবিবারের free Speaking Live". No fee figures: the cost card
  says "পুরো test আবার দেওয়ার খরচ" vs "শুধু একটা skill আবার দেওয়ার খরচ" with a placeholder
  `[fee figures: add once the IDP source is approved]`. No score promise.
- **Call to action:** "Save করে রাখো। কোন module-এ আটকে আছো, comment-এ লেখো।"
- **Source:** targeting hook 4, angle 1; research: Top findings #1, Niche news (One Skill Retake)
- **Facts to check before posting:**
  - One Skill Retake rules (within 60 days, computer-delivered only) were **not re-verified**
    (research: Gaps). Check on ielts.org (approved source) before posting.
  - Don't say "paper test is gone" in general (research: Niche news, correction).
  - Fee figures held back until the IDP Bangladesh page is approved as a source.

### tue-01-reel
- **Pillar / theme:** Authority / open
- **Persona and pain point:** P1, no time between university mid-terms and IELTS prep
- **Hook:** Mid-term চলছে, IELTS-ও সামনে? দিনে ২০ মিনিটে Speaking প্র্যাকটিস, চলো দেখি কীভাবে।
- **Idea:** Coach to camera, 30–45s: a 20-minute daily Speaking routine that fits around classes
  (e.g. 5 min Part 1 questions aloud, 10 min one Part 2 cue card with a 1-minute plan and 2-minute
  answer recorded on the phone, 5 min listen back and note one fix). Practice prompts in English,
  explained in Bangla. Light, low-volume music allowed.
- **Call to action:** "রবিবার রাতের Live-এ এই routine একসাথে করবো। Comment-এ 'Live' লেখো।"
- **Source:** targeting hook 6; research: Audience questions ("klk mid"), Occasions (mid-terms)
- **Facts to check before posting:** Speaking part timings (Part 2: 1 minute to prepare, up to
  2 minutes to speak) against ielts.org test format.

### wed-01-carousel
- **Pillar / theme:** Authority / open
- **Persona and pain point:** P1 (also P3, and P2-leaning per targeting), last-week panic with no plan

### thu-01-reel
- **Pillar / theme:** Authority / open (runs memory Open experiment #3, figure-free)

  alternative to the crowded "retake wastes money" ads: a free check, no score promise. "লাগবে ৭"
  stays generic (no reason or institution named).
- **Call to action:** "Free mock test বুক করতে comment-এ 'Check' লেখো, inbox-এ জানিয়ে দেবো।"
- **Source:** targeting hook 3, angle 3; research: Top findings #3, Active ads (Pattern);
  memory: Open experiments #3

  next score will rise.
