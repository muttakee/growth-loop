## Patterns seen across competitors (from their websites, not their posts)

- **Free mock test as the entry offer.** Mentors', FutureEd and 10 Minute School all promote free
  mock tests [unb.com.bd link above; https://futureed.org/ielts-mock-test; 10MS course snippet].
  This is the same funnel as our brief's main offer, so ours must stand out through the **live
  Facebook speaking** format and the coach's personal feedback (inferred).
- **"In Bangla" is now a common selling point** (10MS book title, FutureEd "Bangla-friendly",
  OneIELTS "Banglay IELTS"). On its own it is not a USP any more (inferred).
- **Community add-ons:** Facebook support group (10MS), Telegram group (OneIELTS) [links above].
- **Price anchors in the market:** BDT 15,000 offline course (10MS English Centre), BDT 5,000–8,500
  promotional courses (FutureEd, older offer), against our BDT 6,500 online batch and BDT 3,000 crash
  course [links above]. The test itself now costs BDT 31,100 (BDT 34,850 UKVI) per attempt
  [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update].
- **Caution:** a local institute advertises "Bandscore Goal: 6.5+ Guaranteed"
  [https://tafe.bubt.edu.bd/?p=26972]. Our brief bans guaranteed band promises. Never imitate this.
