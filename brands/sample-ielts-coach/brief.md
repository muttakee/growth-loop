## Profile

### Mission

Help Bangladeshi students reach their target IELTS band on the first attempt, with simple Bangla
explanations and real exam practice, so they don't waste money on retakes.

### Vision

To be the most trusted Bangla-speaking IELTS teacher on Facebook for students planning to study
or work abroad.

### Next year goal

Get 300 paid students into the online IELTS batch, and grow the Facebook profile to 20,000 followers.

### USP

- 8 years of teaching, with an average student result of band 7.
- Every concept is explained in simple Bangla, with English examples.
- A free weekly speaking mock test on Facebook Live.

### Target customer

University students and young professionals in Bangladesh, aged 19–30, who need IELTS for higher
study, migration or work abroad.

### Customer persona

A final-year university student in Dhaka who wants to study in Canada or the UK. They need
band 6.5–7. They're nervous about Speaking and Writing, have little time, and worry about the cost
of coaching and of a retake.

### Business product

- **Online IELTS batch:** 8 weeks, 3 live classes a week, BDT 6,500.
- **Speaking and Writing crash course:** 3 weeks, BDT 3,000.
- **Free:** weekly speaking mock test on Facebook Live.
- **Main offer:** book a free mock test, then join the batch.

### Target customer geography

Bangladesh: mainly Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet. Also Bangladeshi students already abroad.

### Professional info (personal profile only)

- IELTS trainer for 8 years. Former English lecturer at a private university (fictional).
- MA in English (fictional).
- More than 2,000 students taught. Own band score: 8.5 (fictional).

### Personal info (personal profile only)

Grew up outside Dhaka and learned English without coaching, so he understands students from
non-English-medium backgrounds. Values honesty: no fake band promises. Enjoys cricket and tea.

## Content rules

### Language

- **Main language for captions, hooks, voiceovers and calls to action:** Bangla.
- **Content that stays in another language:** IELTS learning material (sample answers, vocabulary,
  example sentences, test questions) stays in English and is explained in Bangla.
- **Quality rule:** must sound natural, not machine-translated.

### Voice

Friendly elder-brother teacher: warm, encouraging and practical. Short sentences.
Use: "চিন্তা করো না", "চলো দেখি". Avoid: guaranteed band promises, shaming students' English.

### Visuals and audio

- **Logo placement:** bottom-right corner, small
- **Colours and fonts:** navy `#1E3A5F`, orange `#F28C28`, white. Bangla font: Hind Siliguri.
  English font: Poppins.
- **Music:** allowed (light, low volume)
- **People and figures:** real person (the coach). No animated figures.
- **Preferred visuals:** clean, bold text, coach photo in the corner, exam-style cards.

### Approved sources

- Official IELTS website (ielts.org) for test format and band descriptors.
- Cambridge IELTS books for practice questions (cite book and test number).

### No-go topics

- No guaranteed band scores or visa promises.
- No criticism of other teachers or coaching centres by name.
- No political or religious content.
- No leaked or "real exam" questions.
