# Personas: Sample IELTS Coach

> Written by the Targeting Strategist, and updated as we learn.
> First version written by the Onboarding agent (2026-11-01), based on the brief's Target customer
> and Customer persona plus public sources.
>
> **Limit on "Words they use":** real Facebook/Instagram comments could not be read during
> onboarding (facebook.com and instagram.com were not reachable). The Bangla phrases below come from
> Bangla press articles about IELTS preparation (headlines and trainer quotes), not verbatim student
> comments. Phrases marked `(inferred)` are typical wording, not seen in a source. The Researcher
> should replace them with real comment phrases from competitor posts as soon as possible.

## Persona 1: Final-year "study abroad" student (core persona from the brief)

- **Who:** 21–24, final-year private or public university student in Dhaka. Plans a Master's (or
  Bachelor's top-up) in Canada or the UK. Has never taken IELTS. [brief: Customer persona]
- **Goal:** Band 6.5–7 on the first attempt, in time for intake deadlines. UK postgraduate courses
  typically ask 6.5–7.0 and need IELTS for UKVI [https://gabble.ai/blog/study-in-uk-for-bangladeshi-students].
  Canada has no fixed study-permit minimum since the SDS stream ended in Nov 2024, so each university
  sets the score [https://gostudyin.com/bangladesh/study-in-canada/study-guides].
- **Fears and pain points:** Speaking and Writing [brief]. Gets nervous and speaks in broken,
  word-by-word sentences even after 12 years of English at school
  [https://www.prothomalo.com/education/examination/26znz7bure]. Many students find Speaking Part 3
  the hardest [https://bangla.thedailystar.net/youth/education/news-500221]. Little time because of
  final-year exams and thesis [brief]. Cost: the test is BDT 31,100 (UKVI BDT 34,850)
  [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update], so a failed attempt is expensive.
  Confused about Academic vs UKVI vs General (inferred).
- **Words they use:** "আইইএলটিএস প্রস্তুতি", "ইংরেজি বলায় জড়তা"
  [https://www.prothomalo.com/education/examination/26znz7bure]; "ঘরে বসে IELTS প্রস্তুতি"
  [book title: https://othoba.com/ghore-boshe-ielts-prostuti-by-munzereen-shahid-book-vandar-521695];
  "speaking e bhoy lage", "band 7 kivabe pabo", "UKVI lagbe?" (inferred).
- **When they're online:** Late evening to after midnight, about 9pm–1am, and between classes
  (inferred; no Bangladesh time-of-day data found). 18–24 is Bangladesh's largest Facebook age
  group, at 31.4M users [https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-bangladesh/2024/01/].
- **Best hooks:** "Speaking এ ভয় লাগে? চলো দেখি কেন" style fear-to-fix hooks; "Part 3 এ এই ভুলটা
  সবাই করে" mistake lists; a 30-second clip of a live mock-test answer improved on the spot
  (inferred; must follow the brief's voice and no-promise rules).
- **What makes them buy:** A free speaking mock test where they hear their own weak points [brief:
  main offer]. Seeing peers like them improve. A price below offline centres (BDT 6,500 against a
  BDT 15,000 offline course [https://cdn.10minuteschool.com/images/Brochure_10MS_English_Centre_1715256964842.pdf]).
  Live classes that fit around university (inferred).
- **Updates (2026-W46):**
  - **Fee caveat:** the BDT 31,100 / UKVI 34,850 figure above is not confirmed. Sources conflict:
    BDT 24,950 [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/ielts-exam-fee], 28,450 / UKVI 27,450
    [https://www.idp.com/bangladesh/ielts/prepare-for-ielts/test-fee], 29,600 (BC Dhaka, Oct sessions)
    [https://speakshark.com/blog/ielts-exam-dates-october-2026-bangladesh], 31,100
    [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update] (all via search snippets). Do not quote a fee
    until checked on the official booking page. [research 2026-W46: Top 3 findings #2]
  - **Test format:** IELTS in Bangladesh is computer-delivered only; paper test not offered after
    31 Jan 2026 [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/which-test-do-i-take/paper-based-ielts, via
    search snippet]; UKVI is fully digital [https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery,
    via search snippet]. First-timers will meet an on-screen test, including typed Writing (typing
    pressure is inferred). [research 2026-W46: Top 3 findings #1]
  - **Timing:** UK January 2027 intake deadlines fall in November (e.g. LJMU CAS deadline 30 Nov 2026
    [https://cm-prod.ljmu.ac.uk/campaigns/january-intake-2027]; Kent application deadline 27 Nov,
    year inferred [https://www.gradding.com/blog/study-abroad/january-intake-in-the-uk, via search
    snippet]). [research 2026-W46: Top 3 findings #3]

## Persona 2: Young professional heading for work or migration

- **Who:** 25–30, employed (bank, garments, IT, NGO) in Dhaka, Chattogram or Sylhet. Wants to
  migrate or work abroad (Canada PR, UK, Australia). Takes IELTS **General Training**, which is the
  version for work and migration [https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2025/09/15/Bangladesh/SUP/5_11/2593da2b4341e36b_11.pdf].
  [brief: Target customer, geography]
- **Goal:** A high score, especially in Speaking. For Canada, band 7 clearly meets Federal Skilled
  Worker needs and higher Speaking scores add CRS points
  [https://ielts.idp.com/bangladesh/about/news-and-articles/article-what-does-band-7-mean-anyway].
- **Fears and pain points:** No time after office hours. Out of practice with academic English.
  Speaking is often the weakest module (a Canada-focused prep article says this about Indian
  applicants; seen only in a search snippet, exact page not confirmed among
  [https://ieltsidpindia.com/blog/top-7-tips-to-score-high-ielts-band-for-canada] and
  [https://www.pw.live/exams/ielts/ielts-preparation-tips-for-canada-immigration]; that it applies to
  Bangladeshi applicants is inferred). Worried that one
  missed score will delay the whole migration file (inferred).
- **Words they use:** "General IELTS", "CRS score", "Canada PR er jonno koto band lage", "office er
  pore kokhon porbo" (inferred; no sourced comments yet).
- **When they're online:** After work and after dinner, about 9pm–12am; Friday and Saturday
  daytime (inferred).
- **Best hooks:** "Office এর পরে দিনে ৩০ মিনিটে Speaking প্রস্তুতি"; "General vs Academic: কোনটা
  তোমার লাগবে?"; "Band 7 মানে আসলে কী" (explained from ielts.org band descriptors, an approved
  source) (inferred).
- **What makes them buy:** The 3-week Speaking and Writing crash course (BDT 3,000) fits a busy
  schedule [brief: products]. Evening live classes, recordings, and clear honest advice without
  visa promises (inferred).

## Persona 3: The retaker who missed by half a band

- **Who:** 20–28, anywhere in Bangladesh or a Bangladeshi student already abroad [brief: geography].
  Has taken IELTS once and got 6.0 or 6.5 with one weak module (usually Writing or Speaking) (inferred).
- **Goal:** Lift one module by 0.5–1 band on the next attempt, fast. IDP describes paid speaking
  coaching as aimed at exactly this "boost by half a band" need
  [https://ieltsjp.com/japan/prepare/paid-practice-materials/ielts-speaking-coaching/en-gb].
- **Fears and pain points:** Paying BDT 31,100 again [https://mentors.com.bd/news/ielts-test-fee-update],
  and failing again. Feels they "underperformed": a Bangla Daily Star article notes most candidates
  score below their real ability, mainly because of fear
  [https://bangla.thedailystar.net/youth/education/news-500221]. Embarrassed to tell family or
  friends (inferred).
- **Words they use:** "যোগ্যতার তুলনায় কম স্কোর" (paraphrased from
  [https://bangla.thedailystar.net/youth/education/news-500221]); "abar exam dite hobe", "writing e
  6 ashe", "0.5 er jonno atke gesi" (inferred).
- **When they're online:** Late night, about 10pm–1am, often while searching for a quick fix
  (inferred).
- **Best hooks:** "Writing এ ৬ আটকে আছে? এই ৩টা জিনিস দেখো"; "Retake এর আগে নিজের ভুলগুলো জানো";
  before/after rewrites of a Task 2 paragraph (Cambridge book, test number cited) (inferred).
- **What makes them buy:** Specific, personal feedback on their weak module; the free mock test to
  find exactly where they lose marks; the crash course price compared with the cost of another
  test (inferred). The brief's honesty ("no fake band promises") builds trust with this group,
  because they have already been let down once (inferred).
- **Updates (2026-W46):**
  - **One Skill Retake:** British Council launched One Skill Retake in Bangladesh (retake one
    section, not the whole test)
    [https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/education/news/british-council-ielts-launches-one-skill-retake-bangladesh-3428586].
    Computer-delivered tests only, booked within 60 days of the original test
    [https://www.ielts.org/one-skill-retake, via search snippet]. Not every centre may offer it
    [https://vocal.media/education/ielts-registration-and-requirements-in-bangladesh, via search snippet].
    Fee not found. This changes this persona's options: "pay for the whole test again" is no longer
    the only path (inferred). [research 2026-W46: Top 3 findings #1; Niche news #2]
  - **Fee caveat:** the BDT 31,100 figure above is not confirmed (sources conflict; see Persona 1
    update, 2026-W46). Do not quote a fee until checked on the official booking page.
  - **Nerves:** nervous candidates speak too fast, then speed up further after noticing mistakes,
    or say too little, which lowers the score
    [https://www.awalenglish.com/ielts-preparation/overcoming-ielts-speaking-nervousness/;
    https://ted-ielts.com/8-useful-ielts-speaking-tips/, via search snippets; prep sites, not
    audience comments]. [research 2026-W46: Audience questions #1]
